# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Vietnamese high school students (Grade 12) navigating university admission decisions. Context: stressed, time-pressured, researching on phone and desktop. Job: understand their score → find reachable schools → get confident decisions. Secondary users: university advisor staff (manage consultations) and platform admins (moderate content).

## Product Purpose

A university admission guidance platform that lets students input grades (manual or OCR), compare against past admission scores, receive AI-powered major recommendations, and engage with a community forum. Success = student feels informed and confident, not overwhelmed.

## Brand Personality

**Confident + Trustworthy + Approachable.** The interface should feel like a knowledgeable older sibling helping — authoritative but never cold or corporate. Voice is direct and clear, not academic.

## Anti-references

- Generic SaaS dashboards with identical icon+heading+text card grids
- AI-scaffold landing pages (eyebrow kickers "ABOUT / PROCESS / PRICING", numbered section markers)
- Corporate banking navy-and-gold (stiff, impersonal)
- Saturated warm-tinted cream backgrounds (AI slop default)
- Playfair Display headings paired with Inter body — that pairing reads as default AI output

## Design Principles

1. **Show the work, not the chrome.** Data and content are the hero. Navigation and UI chrome step back.
2. **Confidence through clarity.** Every number, label, and action should be unambiguous. No decorative ambiguity.
3. **Speed is a feature.** Transitions are fast (150-300ms). Motion clarifies, never decorates.
4. **Warmth in the details.** Rounded edges, humanist typography, gold accents — the approachable touch without being childish.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA minimum. `prefers-reduced-motion` respected — no animation plays if user has opted out. Color contrast verified on all text/background combinations. Font sizes support readable body text for students reading longform content.
